Tanker Desk Mail

Tanker Desk Mail is a private internal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

  • The app requests read-only access to Gmail and Google Drive of the owner's own accounts.
  • Data is used only to search and read the owner's own mail and files on the owner's computer.
  • Data is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties and is not used for advertising.
  • The app cannot send, change or delete mail or files.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
